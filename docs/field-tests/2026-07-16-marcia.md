# Field Test #1 — Marcia (ICP; July 16, 2026)

First external ICP field test, via email. Tester profile: exactly the ICP — 70s, NYC, and the
author of a hand-made paper family planner (whose table of contents already shaped the emergency
guide's sections). She independently searched for competitors (Everplans, Sortly, HomeZada) and
found the same three-camp gap the competitive research documented — customer-verified positioning.

## Findings → dispositions (all six accepted; reviewed by a 7-agent panel)

## What her item list taught us
Her first items — sofa, African mask, dish cabinet — are story-dense and market-value-light, unlike
the jewelry-heavy demo assumptions. Added: `Rugs & Textiles` and `Décor & Cultural Pieces`
categories (with market profiles + icons), a no-price-guess variant in the identification shape
(the honest answer for a sofa is a shrug, not a number), and "The Kitchen Table" in the seed —
worth almost nothing, the most valuable thing in the house.

## Meta-lesson
One email from one real ICP tester found a data-loss bug, a trust-model violation, and a
life-safety copy gap that five multi-agent reviews missed. Field tests with real users on their
real things are the highest-yield review available. Protocol for the next round: `protocol.md`.
