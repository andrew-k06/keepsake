# Keepsake Field Test — ICP Protocol v1

60–90 minute session + 2-week diary. Tester's own device, own home, own things. No demo binder
shown first. An observer (family member is fine) writes timestamps and does not help unless asked —
every ask-for-help is logged as a finding.

## Session tasks (in order, no coaching)

1. **Cold open** — "Set this up for yourself." Measure: is the concept understood *before* entering
   My Binder? Note the first screen where it clicks.
2. **First three items, their choice** — no category prompts. Record what they pick (feeds the
   category ledger), photo-first vs name-first, voice use, and any item/story vocabulary confusion.
3. **The papers task** — "Record where your will and power of attorney are, and who has copies."
   Measure: emergency guide vs binder reach; do "located"/date-prepared read correctly; and the
   must-be-zero check — does anything make them believe family could *access* the directive in time
   through the app?
4. **The reports task** — "Print what you'd hand your attorney; print what you'd hand your
   insurance agent." They check the output against their actual facts. (Highest yield per minute —
   field test #1 caught two report defects here.) Measure factual errors and whether they can say
   who each report is for.
5. **The decline task** — offer a reason to stop ("this is a lot, isn't it?"). Measure: does "Not
   today" behavior feel respectful on return?

## Diary (2 weeks)
Unprompted returns (count + trigger), whether they showed it to anyone (the conversation is the
endgame), what they added when no one was watching.

## Instrumentation
The audit log + `preparedness.steps` timestamps suffice for n≤5. No new telemetry.

## Success bar (per tester)
- Concept understood before My Binder
- ≥3 real items, ≥1 with a story
- Zero factually wrong statements in any printed report
- Zero beliefs that Keepsake substitutes for distributed document copies
- Unprompted return within 14 days

## Recruiting
3–5 testers of ICP age from OUTSIDE the family (field-test-#1's tester can nominate — the
paper-planner peer group is exactly right), plus one adult-child pair for Together mode.
