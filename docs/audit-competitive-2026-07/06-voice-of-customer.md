# Voice-of-Customer + Business-Model Research

*Six-agent audit & competitive session, July 2026. See 00-synthesis.md. Caveats in §5.*

## 1. COMPLAINT MINING

**A. Abandonment — "Mom never finished it" (story apps).** Reddit-pattern on StoryWorth: parents "didn't answer a single question for a year"; Reviewopedia verbatim: "My subscription expired before I answered all of my questions and unless I pay $60 to renew for a year, I don't get the book" — the product punishes non-completion with more payment. Also lost stories, random formatting, poor support on misprints. ([Reviewopedia](https://www.reviewopedia.com/storyworth-reviews), [Thingtesting](https://thingtesting.com/brands/storyworth/reviews))

**B. Subscription resentment + auto-renew traps.** StoryWorth auto-renew is the top complaint theme — it now runs defensive "we don't auto-renew" ads. Remento à-la-carte gotchas: **$50 to export your own PDF**, $99/extra storyteller, $69/extra book. Trust & Will BBB: cards "locked inside their site." Home inventory: "no monthly fees" called out as unserved demand. Seniors: subscription-cancellation is standard fixed-income budgeting advice (NCOA). ([Memoirji](https://memoirji.com/blog/storyworth-pricing-2026-buyer-guide/), [Remento pricing](https://help.remento.co/en/articles/8365892-remento-s-pricing-guide), [T&W BBB](https://www.bbb.org/us/ca/san-diego/profile/legal-information-services/trust-will-1126-1000057285/complaints), [HomeProof](https://homeproofvault.com/blog/best-home-inventory-apps-compared/))

**C. Data lock-in / "what if the company dies".** **Remento**: Trustpilot reviewers describe being **locked out of stories they created once the subscription lapses**. **SafeBeyond actually died** (2022): users got 30 days to download before servers went dark. **Everplans acquired twice**; its ToS concedes "in case we do [shut down]…". **Centriq shut down Jan 2026 and deleted all user data.** Keepsake's local-first/printable/exportable posture answers this directly. ([Trustpilot Remento](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/remento.co), [SafeBeyond](https://digital-danach.de/safebeyond-geht-offline/), [Precoa/Everplans](https://www.funeralvision.com/precoa-acquires-everplans/))

**D. Complexity / senior-hostile design.** AARP: **64% of adults 50+ say technology isn't designed with their age in mind**; top barriers: privacy (~33%), ease of use (20%), setup (19%). Everplans reviews: crashes while entering important info, unclear next steps, unusable iPad app. ([AARP 2026 Tech Trends](https://www.aarp.org/pri/topics/technology/internet-media-devices/2026-technology-trends-older-adults/), [Everplans Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/everplans.com))

**E. Privacy fear as blocker.** Everplans users "uneasy about the amount of personal information requested"; privacy = #1 senior adoption barrier. Keepsake asks for photos of objects, not passwords.

**F. Morbidity aversion.** Estate planning among the most-avoided family topics; Fidelity: 70% of parents believe they've had the estate conversation, **more than half of adult children say it never happened**; Ameriprise: families who do talk find it "smoother than anticipated" — validating the gift-not-death-prep reframe. ([Fidelity](https://www.fidelity.com/about-fidelity/individual-investing/fidelity-study-children-in-dark-about-parents-estate-plans), [Ameriprise](https://newsroom.ameriprise.com/research/ameriprise-study-family-financial-discussions-go-smoother-than-anticipated-but-unrealistic-inheritance-expectations-persist.htm))

**G. Families fighting over stuff.** Attorney consensus: **sentimental items cause more fights than money** ("thousands in fees over knick-knacks"). Newsweek/Reddit jewelry-split case; attorney verdict: "if the mom had put in writing who was receiving which jewels… resolved." ([Byers Elder Law](https://www.byerselderlaw.com/the-use-of-a-personal-property-memorandum-with-your-will-or-trust-to-distribute-your-stuff), [Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/sibling-split-moms-inheritance-worse-deal-jewelry-1814549))

**H. Lost value + scams at disposition.** Estate-sale mispricing is systemic (grieving families on deadlines). FBI Boston: **$26M+ courier-scam losses, 98% from victims over 60**; lowball "we buy gold" tactics, today-only pressure. Validates Before You Sell line-for-line. ([FBI Boston](https://www.fbi.gov/contact-us/field-offices/boston/news/fbi-boston-warns-of-increase-in-gold-bar-and-bulk-cash-courier-scams), [CBS](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/gold-scams-targeting-seniors-red-flags-every-retiree-should-know/))

**I. Regret at story loss.** Two-thirds of adults regret not asking grandparents about their lives; 20% don't know grandmother's maiden name. ([SSAFA survey](https://www.blackpoolgazette.co.uk/read-this/more-than-two-thirds-of-adults-regret-not-talking-to-their-grandparents-about-their-lives-and-memories-before-they-passed-away-3407285))

## 3. DEMAND SIGNALS

- **Cerulli (2025): $124T transferring through 2048** ($79T from boomers+); older households hold 61% of US wealth. ([Cerulli](https://www.cerulli.com/press-releases/cerulli-anticipates-124-trillion-in-wealth-will-transfer-through-2048))
- **Preparedness falling while assets grow:** only **24% of Americans have a will, down from 33% in 2022**; #1 reason procrastination. ([Caring.com](https://www.caring.com/resources/wills-survey))
- **Double downsizing wave:** two generations downsizing simultaneously; "Great Boomer Declutter" named in press; brown-furniture collapse sharpens "what's actually worth something?"
- **Swedish death cleaning** sustained mainstream momentum (book + Peacock series).
- **Insurance gap:** only **47% of homeowners have a home inventory** (Triple-I). ([III](https://www.iii.org/fact-statistic/facts-statistics-homeowners-and-renters-insurance))
- **Channel capacity:** NASMM 1,000+ senior-move managers anticipating the surge.
- Dense competitor attack-SEO indicates high comparison-shopping volume.

## 4. THE CAPITALIZE LIST (ranked by how directly the existing build answers each)

1. **"Who gets what" undocumented; sentimental items cause the worst fights; nobody makes the memorandum** → per-item wishes + memorandum export + printable summary. Core product; no competitor pairs item + story + wish + document.
2. **Subscription fatigue / auto-renew traps; no buy-once exists** → $129 buy-once; sub only child-paid. Structural answer, already the pricing page.
3. **Data lock-in ($50 PDF ransom, 30-day shutdowns, deleted data)** → on-device, free print/exports. Market it: "your binder outlives us."
4. **Story-capture abandonment (52-week programs, writing burden)** → object-anchored micro-stories by voice; the item in hand is the prompt; no deadline.
5. **Value blindness at disposition; appraisals price out whole-house triage** → sold-comp ranges + preflight triage incl. "you don't need to pay anyone."
6. **Senior-targeted selling scams (FBI: 98% of losses 60+)** → Before You Sell. No competitor has anything like it.
7. **Tech not designed for seniors (AARP 64%)** → senior-first tokens, voice-first, plain-words confidence.
8. **Morbidity aversion blocks the category; the talk goes fine once started** → gift framing, wishes-not-bequests, child-initiated onboarding.
9. **53% have no home inventory; inventory apps are cold, pro-priced** → professional inventory export falls out of the binder free.
10. **Privacy fear (vaults demand passwords up front)** → EXIF stripping, values hidden in print, dormant trusted contact; photos of objects, not passwords.
