# Competitive Landscape: Digital Estate Organization / Legacy Planning

*Six-agent audit & competitive session, July 2026. See 00-synthesis.md. Pricing verified on vendor sites where noted.*

Keepsake's territory for overlap scoring: **belongings/heirlooms with stories · item-level wishes · senior-first UX · voice capture · valuation + scam shield · child-buys-for-parent gifting motion**.

---

## 1. Competitor Profiles

### Everplans — the document vault incumbent
- Guided digital vault for estate documents, financial accounts, passwords, medical wishes, funeral preferences; "deputies" get trigger-based access. ([everplans.com/digital-estate](https://www.everplans.com/digital-estate))
- **Pricing (verified):** free = 3 items; Premium **$99.99/yr**. ([pricing](https://www.everplans.com/pricing))
- Founded 2012, ~$16.4M raised, acquired by National Guardian Life 2021, then by funeral pre-need company **Precoa Oct 2024** — now a distribution asset, not a consumer product company.
- **vs Keepsake:** documents-first. No item photos-with-stories, no voice, no valuation, no item-level wishes. Reviewers call sharing "constrained"; two acquisitions signal stalled consumer growth.

### Trust & Will — legal documents at scale
- State-specific wills/trusts/POA/directives; Will $199/$299 couple; Trust $499/$599; $19–39/yr updates; attorney support $299. Series C $34M+ (2025); **exclusive AARP estate-planning benefit**. ([trustandwill.com/compare](https://trustandwill.com/compare), [AARP partnership](https://trustandwill.com/partnerships/aarp))
- **vs Keepsake:** the legal layer Keepsake deliberately isn't. Personal property = a schedule of assets, not storied objects. A natural **partner/export target** for the attorney memorandum.

### Cake (joincake) → Afterall — exited the category
- Free end-of-life checklists; **acquired Sept 2024 by Foundation Partners Group** (250+ funeral homes), folded into funeral-services front end. Evidence free-checklist models don't sustain a consumer business.

### Prisidio — the AARP-endorsed vault
- Secure vault (100 GB), photos and video notes, Co-Owner/Keyholder roles. **$150/yr** retail (verified); **AARP members ~$20/yr** as the AARP Digital Vault. ([prisidio.com/plans](https://www.prisidio.com/plans), [AARP](https://help.aarp.org/s/article/how-to-get-aarp-digital-vault))
- **vs Keepsake:** MEDIUM overlap — senior channel + video notes; but a file cabinet, not a story-and-wishes binder. The $20 AARP price signals commoditization of pure vault storage.

### GoodTrust — cheap docs + digital legacy
- Docs generator + vault + digital-account memorialization. Estate+ **$149 one-time** then $39/yr. ~$8.15M raised. ([mygoodtrust.com/pricing](https://mygoodtrust.com/pricing))
- **vs Keepsake:** LOW-MED. Breadth-over-depth; no belongings/stories/valuation layer.

### Clocr — digital-assets niche
- Digital estate for online accounts; ~$59.99/yr; crowdfunded, small. LOW overlap; blockchain pitch is the opposite of senior-trust language.

### Nokbox — the physical binder proof-of-demand
- Physical file-box system: 15 categories, 67 folders, 72 checklists. **$169** original; $59 Lite; $179 fireproof. ([thenokbox.com](https://www.thenokbox.com/))
- Founded 2021, bootstrapped; **$60M+ sold, 500,000+ families claimed** (company figures, unverified). ([Our Story](https://www.thenokbox.com/pages/our-story))
- **vs Keepsake:** HIGH on buyer motion — the best evidence that **adult children pay ~$130–180 one-time, often as a gift, for exactly this job**. Zero on digital: paper goes stale, can't hold stories/photos/values, no sharing, no scam shield. Keepsake's printed binder produces the Nokbox artifact as an *output* of a living system.

### LastingMatters — the workbook
- 170-page printed organizer, $29/$20 PDF; NAEPC channel. LOW overlap; reviews call it overwhelming.

### Empathy — the post-loss B2B2C giant
- Bereavement/estate-settlement + LifeVault pre-need planning, via life insurers/employers. **$162M raised** ($72M Series C, May 2025); 8 of top 10 US life carriers. ([FinTech Global](https://fintech.global/2025/05/30/bereavement-support-fintech-empathy-lands-72m-to-expand-ai-driven-lifevault-platform/))
- **vs Keepsake:** LOW-MED — arrives after death via B2B2C; no belongings layer. Most credible future threat if it goes direct-to-consumer.

### Artifcts — the closest product to Keepsake's core
- Photograph object + story (photo/video/**audio**), QR stickers link physical object to record; exports "for wills and insurance"; **free valuations via Heritage Auctions** for paid members; printed-book partner; patent 11,816,189. ([artifcts.com](https://artifcts.com/))
- Pricing: free = 5 artifacts; ~$45+/yr; Professional $99+/yr (page may be cached; re-verify).
- Founded 2021 (ex-CIA founders); TIME/AP/Forbes coverage; no disclosed VC round found.
- **vs Keepsake:** **HIGH** — stories behind objects, audio, valuation partner, estate exports. Weaknesses: collector/organizer positioning not senior-first guided binder; **no item-level wishes as a first-class feature**; no scam protection; no emergency guide; no child-gifts-parent flow; subscription-only.

### FairSplit — the fairness fight, post-mortem
- Inventories + structured **blind multi-round division** (points bidding, snake draft) for estates/divorce. **$179.95–$289.95** per event. ([fairsplit.com](https://www.fairsplit.com/))
- **vs Keepsake:** HIGH on "who gets what" but used **after the owner is gone** — the owner's voice, story, and wishes are absent. Adversarial framing vs Keepsake's preventive, loving framing.

### SaveOr — AI home inventory drifting into estate
- AI photo-ID inventory with estimated values; family members **view, claim, annotate items**; assign to heirs; **exports a Personal Property Memorandum**; markets downsizing/estate sales/"senior transitions". Pricing behind trial — unverified. ([saveor.com/estate-planning](https://www.saveor.com/estate-planning))
- **vs Keepsake:** HIGH on mechanics, LOW on soul: inventory-first, no voice-story emphasis, no senior-first design, no scam shield; the heir-"claim" motion can trigger the fairness fight rather than resolve it with the parent's recorded intent.

### Adjacent — worth tracking
- **Trustworthy** ("Family Operating System"): $120–480/yr, affluent-family docs hub. MED.
- **Carefull**: financial safety for aging adults — account monitoring, ScamCheck, $19.99/mo; $16.5M raised. Proves the child-pays-to-protect-parent wallet; knows nothing about belongings. ([getcarefull.com](https://getcarefull.com/))
- **Airloom** (airloom.digital): heirloom + 5 guided voice questions, "built so our parents could use it" — tiny/early.
- **Family Heirloom Archive** (iOS): photo + story + recipient + value — solo-dev utility, no family system.
- **Buried in Work CLEAR Kits**: Nokbox competitor, physical; prices unverified.
- **The Postage**: $5.99–9.99/mo legacy vault; domain didn't resolve — status unverified.

## 3. The 3 Closest — where each stops short

**1. Artifcts** — shares the soul. Stops short at: (a) no item-level wish/recipient designation as a structured, printable intent record; (b) collector positioning, not senior-first guided path; (c) no protection layer between grandma and a lowball offer; (d) subscription-only, no gift SKU, buyer and user not separately designed for.

**2. SaveOr** — shares the mechanics. Stops short at: (a) inventory-first, story-last, no voice-primary capture; (b) the "claim" model invites the fairness fight instead of centering the owner's recorded wish; (c) no senior UX, scam shield, or gift-book artifact; (d) opaque pricing behind a card-required trial.

**3. Nokbox** (FairSplit as the post-mortem shadow) — owns the exact purchase motion and validates binder-as-gift at ~$169 one-time. Stops short at: paper — no photos, stories, voice, values, updates, sharing, or verdicts; decays the day it's filled. FairSplit handles who-gets-what only after the owner can no longer speak — Keepsake's whole premise is capturing that voice while it's here.

## 4. Category Gaps Nobody Serves Well

1. **Item-level story + wish in one record, in the owner's voice.** Nobody produces "this is the clock, here is Mom saying why it matters, and here is who she wants to have it" as a single durable artifact.
2. **Pre-empting the fairness fight (vs adjudicating it).** FairSplit monetizes the fight after death; nobody helps the living parent surface conflicts early and record reasoning. Completely open.
3. **Belongings-aware scam protection.** Carefull watches bank accounts; nobody connects an item's sold-comp value to the offer in grandma's hand with a face-saving script. "Before You Sell" has no competitor.
4. **The senior-driven vs child-driven split.** Every product picks one user; none designs the two-sided motion (child pays + reads; parent authors + controls) with gift onboarding and trusted-contact protocols.
5. **Honest, plain-language valuation for ordinary things.** Hype AI point-values on one side, 2–3-week formal appraisals on the other; nobody does ranges-in-words + "you don't need to pay" + kind decline + accredited routing in one senior-legible flow.
6. **Buy-once pricing in a subscription sea.** Only Nokbox (paper) and GoodTrust (docs) sell ownership; every digital organizer is $100–150/yr forever. $129-once + optional child-paid $79/yr is structurally unoccupied.
7. **The printable family binder as output, not input.** Physical and digital are separate industries; nobody generates the printed binder + attorney-ready memorandum from a living system.
